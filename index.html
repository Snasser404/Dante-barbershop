Our story

More Than a Haircut

Dante opened his first chair in 2014 with a simple belief: a great cut is part craft, part conversation. A decade on, the shop has grown — but the philosophy hasn't changed.

We're old-school where it counts: straight razors, hot towels, and barbers who actually listen. We're modern where it matters: online booking, contemporary styling, and an atmosphere that's as much a retreat as it is a barbershop.

  • Master barbers with 5+ years on the chair
  • Premium products & single-use blades
  • A drink on the house while you wait
